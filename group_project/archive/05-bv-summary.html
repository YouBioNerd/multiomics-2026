Study B — one-page summary: is bacterial vaginosis more common in one arm?

The full workings live in 03-bv-by-arm.qmd; the community-composition context comes from 02-community-composition.qmd. This page is the version to present: the answer, the figure and table that carry it, and the one-liner for the slides.

The answer

No. BV affects roughly 43–49% of participants in every arm, and the differences between arms are what chance produces (Fisher p ≈ 0.45). No contraceptive method in this study is associated with more or less BV.

Why this matters for the other question

Yesterday’s ordination found BV status is the dominant structure in these communities (R² ≈ 13%, p = 0.001) while arm explains almost nothing (R² ≈ 3%, p ≈ 0.1). If BV had clustered in one arm, that arm’s community difference would have had a boring explanation. It did not: BV is spread evenly, so it is a rival explanation for community patterns but not a confounder of the arm comparison — and the suggestive pill-vs-injectable signal from 02-community-composition cannot be explained away as “the pill arm simply had less BV”. (If anything, the pill arm had the least.)

The one-liner for the slides

BV affects 43–49% of participants in every arm (Fisher p ≈ 0.45); the labels validate against Nugent scores and Gardnerella abundance. No method is associated with more or less BV — so BV, the main driver of community composition, is a rival explanation but not a confounder.

Choices made

  • All methods are inherited from 03-bv-by-arm.qmd; nothing was re-decided here — this page only re-presents them.
  • Main test Fisher’s exact test, chosen because two table cells held fewer than 5 samples; chi-square agreed (p ≈ 0.47 / 0.84).
  • Ran the test both ways — three BV categories, and positive-vs-not; the alternative was to pick one and hope.
  • Treated intermediate as not BV in the two-category version; lumping it with positive instead moves the shares to 49% / 46% / 46% and changes nothing.
  • Kept all 93 samples, including the nine low-depth ones: BV status comes from Nugent scoring, not sequencing depth, so shallow samples are still valid rows here.
  • Figures and table recompute their numbers from the raw data rather than copy-pasting, so they would visibly disagree if 03-bv-by-arm.qmd were changed.
